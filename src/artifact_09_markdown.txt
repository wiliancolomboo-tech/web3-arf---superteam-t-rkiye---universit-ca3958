### Incident Response & Operations Runbook

1. **Credit Freeze Procedure (Emergency Stop):**
   - In the event of an MTO fiat rail disruption, call:
     `solana program execute freeze_credit_line <BORROWER_PUBKEY>` via the Risk Admin multisig.
   
2. **Prometheus / Healthcheck Monitoring Endpoint:**
   - The service exposes `/healthz` on port 8080.
   - Status 200 checks Solana RPC latency (< 800ms) and pending transaction queues.

3. **Log Rotation & Audit Ingestion:**
   - Structured JSON logs output to `stdout` are ingested by AWS CloudWatch / Datadog agents:
     `{"level":"INFO","event":"DRAWDOWN_EXECUTED","tx":"5xG9...","time":"2025-02-15T12:00:00Z"}`
